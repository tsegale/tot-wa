City to lodge, hotel to hotel, or a private run into Botswana, South Africa or Zambia. Same driver, same vehicle, for however far the trip actually goes.

Within Namibia, door to door.

Windhoek to Swakopmund, a lodge transfer ahead of a self-drive, or simply a private ride across the city when a taxi won't do. You book the route, we handle the driving.

  • City to city, and city to lodge or reserve
  • One-way or return, scheduled around your plans
  • Can be paired with an airport transfer or a tour on either end

Across the border, paperwork included.

We run private transfers into Botswana, South Africa and Zambia. Border crossings are planned into the route in advance, so the stop at the post is a formality, not a scramble.

  • Botswana, including Kasane and the Chobe region
  • South Africa, northern border crossings
  • Zambia, including the Victoria Falls area

Private transfer

A standard private transfer: the driver completes the route and returns to Windhoek the same day. This covers most point-to-point runs within Namibia.

Overnight supplement

Where the distance means the driver can't get back to Windhoek the same day, an overnight supplement applies to cover the driver's accommodation before or after the transfer.
