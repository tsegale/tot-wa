Where to?

That's what tot wa means in Afrikaans, and it's the first thing we ask every guest, whether they're ten minutes from arrivals pickup or ten days into a loop through the desert.

Started in 2018, as Osona Tours and Transfers.

Tot Wa was founded in 2018 by Etienne Plaatjies, trading at the time as Osona Tours and Transfers. The business kept running airport and private transfers straight through Namibia's Covid-19 lockdowns, when most travel companies simply stopped.

In and out of Hosea Kutako and Eros, city to lodge, and across the border into Botswana, South Africa and Zambia: that groundwork became the foundation for everything that came after it.

Rebranded to Tot Wa in 2021, to build tours around it.

Once Namibia reopened to travel, the business rebranded in 2021 to Tot Wa Tours and Transfers, a deliberate move to focus on tourism alongside the transfer work it was built on.

Six fixed safari routes now, plus anything custom, still driven by the same Namibian team that already knew every road.

Our team

Drivers and guides who know these roads.

Every transfer and every safari is run by a Namibian driver-guide, trained in first aid and defensive driving, and familiar with the route long before your dates are booked. It's the same small team behind every booking, not a rotating pool of subcontractors.

100% Namibian-owned, and country-wide.

Tot Wa is fully Namibian-owned and operated, based in Windhoek with routes running to every region we serve, from the Namib coast to Etosha and the Kunene.

We hire and train locally as standard practice, not a side initiative, because the team who knows these roads best is the team that lives on them.

Our values

What guests can expect, every time.

Security

Tot Wa is committed to ensuring all our clients' safety and security. Our fleet is equipped and maintained to the highest standards, to guarantee roadworthiness and road safety at all times. All our drivers are 1st Aid certified, and every passenger is insured when travelling with Tot Wa.

Teamwork & Communication

At Tot Wa, we are committed to servicing the needs and requests of our clients to the best of our abilities. We strive to effectively and actively communicate with our clients and colleagues to deliver world class transportation services and expertise.

Dependability

The Tot Wa team understands the value of every client's time. We are committed to ensuring that every client's needs and requests are attended to in a timely, hassle free and effective manner. At Tot Wa, we strive to be on time, every time.

Comfort & Cleanliness

At Tot Wa, we ensure that all our clients enjoy a relaxing and comfortable travelling experience every time. The entire Tot Wa fleet is equipped with all the comforts and amenities, and maintained to the highest cleanliness standards to ensure a pleasurable travelling experience.
