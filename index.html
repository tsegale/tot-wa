Built for the airport run. Growing into the open road.

Tot Wa started with transfers: in and out of Hosea Kutako and Eros, city to lodge, and across the border into Botswana, South Africa and Zambia. The name comes from the Afrikaans "tot waar?", meaning until where, and it's the question every one of those journeys starts with.

It turned out to be a good question to build a company around. When guests kept asking us to take them further, into Etosha, out to Twyfelfontein, north to a Himba Living Museum, we started planning full safaris too.
